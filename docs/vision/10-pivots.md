# 10 · Pivot Menu

Strategic options, ranked, with decision triggers. The recommendation is
**P0 with P1 inside it** — but every option below shares the same perception
stack and the same corpus, so switching costs are low by design. The pivots
are postures toward the same technology, not different technologies.

---

## P0 · The hands-free class player (recommended)

**Thesis:** The original vision entered through its feasible face: barre-first,
teacher-mic, curated MIDI library, reflex layer, shadow-mode onboarding,
degradation ladder. Beat the playlist and delete the form
([02 · Reframes](02-reframes.md)).

**Who pays:** the playlist-majority — studios and teachers already paying for
Ballegro/Ballet Class/BalletBox subscriptions plus the interaction tax.

**Effort:** the [90-day plan](11-roadmap.md) reaches the magic-moment demo;
a pilot-grade product is a matter of months beyond, not years.

**Why it leads:** it is the only option that fulfills the actual dream, and
every alternative below remains reachable from it.

## P1 · Syllabus mode (ship first, inside P0)

**Thesis:** RAD / Vaganova / Cecchetti syllabus classes have **fixed
exercises with prescribed music character and known counts**. Perception
collapses to: *which set exercise, which tempo variant, when to start* — an
order of magnitude easier than free-work comprehension, deliverable with
today's pipeline plus the cue detector.

**Who pays:** exam-prep studios and home practice — a market that already
buys syllabus recordings and drills to them.

**Why inside P0:** same rig, same engine, same ladder; syllabus mode is
free-work mode with the priors turned up to certainty. It ships earlier,
funds the harder work, and every syllabus class still feeds calibration and
corpus. **Decision trigger to elevate it to the lead product:** R1/R3
tripwires fire ([09](09-risk-register.md)) — free-work comprehension stalls
across pilot teachers while syllabus demand is proven.

## P2 · The dataset as the contribution

**Thesis:** If the deeper motivation is research: the corpus
([08 · Benchmark](08-benchmark-and-shadow-mode.md)) is a first-of-its-kind
dataset (marking comprehension has *no* prior art — [03](03-landscape.md)
§3.5), the marking-tempo gap has never been quantified, and the onset/accent
methods are novel against both beat-tracking and speech-prosody literatures.
Publishable benchmark + methods paper + eventual fine-tuned small model.

**Why it's not a pivot away:** P0 requires building this anyway. P2 is a
*posture* choice — invest in annotation quality, breadth, and consent for
publication, and the moat doubles as a contribution. **Decision trigger:**
academic collaboration or funding materializes, or a competitor enters and
the dataset's defensive value spikes.

## P3 · The copilot posture (fallback)

**Thesis:** If live-room full autonomy stalls: the same stack drives an
assistant with a human holding start/stop — either the teacher (ladder
rung 3 *as the product*: one tap replaces twenty touchscreen interactions,
tempo/piece/length still inferred) or a human accompanist using it as a
super-instrument (auto-suggested repertoire at inferred parameters).

**Honesty:** this is the weaker vision — but it is a *real product* (the
manual apps prove the willingness to pay for much less), it keeps the data
flywheel spinning, and it is exactly where the ladder lands you gracefully if
rung 1 never earns trust. **Decision trigger:** R8's tripwire — pilots stick
at rung 3 for a full term despite passing gates.

## P4 · Adjacent domains (expansions, not pivots)

Same shape — a rhythmic leader vocalizing structure, then music at exact
parameters:

| Domain | Ease | Catch |
|---|---|---|
| Fitness / barre-fitness classes | Easier (steady BPM culture, counts everywhere) | Music is commodity playlist; less differentiation |
| Figure skating / gymnastics floor | Program music at exact lengths matters | Small, event-driven usage |
| Music education (rhythm/solfège) | The perception stack *is* the product | Different buyer entirely |

Ballet stays the beachhead: hardest signal, richest conventions, clearest
pain, no incumbent. Expansion only after v1 gates pass. (Flourish for the
right moment, in either domain: the engine's MIDI driving a real player
piano — an acoustic instrument visibly playing itself in the studio.)

---

## What never pivots

Whatever the posture: the **perception + precision stack**, the
**`MusicalParameters` contract**, the **corpus**, and the **per-teacher
profiles** carry over unchanged. The pivot menu is cheap *because* the
architecture keeps the intelligence separate from the posture — the same
property (KEEP vs DISPOSABLE) that has already survived three model swaps.
