# 12 · Collaborators & Ecosystem

Who bridges ballet and technology today — candidate collaborators, domain
authorities, institutional vehicles, and the adjacent product ecosystem.

**Method note:** compiled 2026-07 from public professional sources only
(university pages, product sites, press, published work); no personal contact
details are recorded here, and affiliations move — **re-verify before any
outreach**. Confidence tags: entries without a tag were verified against a
cited public source; *(partial)* marks name-public but incomplete background.

  closest technical ancestor. → ismm.ircam.fr
- **Sarah Fdili Alaoui** — Professor, UAL Creative Computing Institute,

  movement-quality features that map onto `QualityProfile`. → casapaganini.unige.it

## 12.6 Ecosystem findings

Three scan results worth keeping in view (full detail in
[03 · Landscape](03-landscape.md)):

1. **Nobody publicly ships AI-generated or teacher-responsive ballet class
   music** (as of 2026-07). The niche remains open.
2. **No ballet accompanist found who has personally built software or
   released a dataset** — the first accompanist-collaborator this project
   converts becomes something the field doesn't yet have.
3. Useful adjacent artifact: **AnnChor** — a labeled video dataset for
   temporal action localization in classical ballet choreography (Springer,
   PR workshops) — relevant to the pose/precision layer.
